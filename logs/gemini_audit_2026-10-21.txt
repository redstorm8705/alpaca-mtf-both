### NEW BUGS FOUND

1. **EXECUTION BUG | MEDIUM | `main.py` & `events/handlers.py` (`preserve_swing_on_shutdown`)**
   - **Description**: Unbreakable process loop on `KeyboardInterrupt` when Swing GTC stop is unresolved.
   - **Code Path**: In `main.py`, the `KeyboardInterrupt` block calls `if not _preserve_swing_on_shutdown(tracker, risk): continue`. In `events/handlers.py`, `_preserve_swing_on_shutdown_once` returns `False` if any issue is present in `summary` (e.g. stop is DAY instead of GTC, or co-held in QHM/F6, or position unmanaged).
   - **Runtime Failure**: When `_preserve_swing_on_shutdown` returns `False`, `main.py` issues `continue` back into `while True:`. When running in foreground or under process supervision, pressing Ctrl+C does not shut down the process; subsequent Ctrl+C presses are caught by the `except KeyboardInterrupt: return False` in `preserve_swing_on_shutdown`, endlessly looping without an exit escape hatch.
   - **Failure Condition**: User issues Ctrl+C to terminate `main.py` while a position in `tracker.open_trades` cannot prove working GTC stop coverage (such as during RTH when stops are DAY orders).

2. **EXECUTION BUG | LOW | `execution/portfolio_tracker.py`**
   - **Description**: Daytrade fills generate spurious `FIFO orphan` warnings in `portfolio_tracker`.
   - **Code Path**: `portfolio_tracker` processes broker fill callbacks without filtering for `daytrade` client_order_id prefixes, attempting to match DRAM daytrade fills against its own swing lot table.
   - **Failure Condition**: Any daytrade execution closing fill triggers `FIFO orphan: closing fill for <SYM> has no prior lot and no tracker record`.
