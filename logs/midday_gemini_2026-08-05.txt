### PERFORMANCE AUDIT
1.  **P&L Sign Inversion for Short Trades (Critical Execution Bug):**
    *   **Description:** The P&L calculations for short positions are inverted. For a short trade, `entry > exit` should result in profit, and `entry < exit` should result in loss. The bot reports the opposite.
    *   **Evidence:**
        *   **TOST:** Entry $32.89, Exit $35.36. As per `ALPACA FILLS TODAY`, this was a short sale (1S). Actual P&L for a short: $32.89 - $35.36 = -$2.47. Reported P&L: (+$2.47).
        *   **AMZN:** Entry $278.72, Exit $272.60. As per `ALPACA FILLS TODAY`, this was a short sale (1S). Actual P&L for a short: $278.72 - $272.60 = +$6.12. Reported P&L: ($-6.12).
    *   **Impact:** This issue leads to inaccurate `Total P&L` (reported $-3.65 vs. actual +$3.65), misrepresents individual trade outcomes, corrupts win rate metrics, and can lead to incorrect risk management decisions (e.g., miscalculating daily loss limits or capital allocation).
2.  **Incorrect Stop Placement for Short Trades (Critical Execution Bug):**
    *   **Description:** For short positions, the protective stop price is placed *below* the entry price, which is characteristic of a stop for a *long* position. A protective stop for a short position should be placed *above* the entry price to limit upside risk.
    *   **Evidence:**
        *   **TOST:** Entry $32.89, Stop $30.66. The stop is $2.23 below the entry.
        *   **AMZN:** Entry $278.72, Stop $251.76. The stop is $26.96 below the entry.
    *   **Impact:** These short positions are either left effectively unprotected from upward price movements or are protected by a stop that would be triggered by further *profit-taking* movement, not loss, which defeats the purpose of a protective stop. The `INTRADAY_STOP_ATR_MULT = 1.2` suggests a stop *magnitude* is calculated correctly, but its *direction* relative to the entry price is inverted for short trades.
3.  **Incorrect Target Placement for Short Trades (Alpha Issue):**
    *   **Description:** For short positions, the profit target prices (T1, T2, T3) are set *above* the entry price. Profit targets for short positions should be *below* the entry price.
    *   **Evidence:**
        *   **TOST:** Entry $32.89. Targets: T1=$33.82, T2=$34.75, T3=$37.54. All targets are above the entry price.
        *   **AMZN:** Entry $278.72. Targets: T1=$289.95, T2=$301.17, T3=$334.85. All targets are above the entry price.
    *   **Impact:** This indicates a fundamental misunderstanding or misconfiguration of short-side profit-taking logic. The bot will never "hit" these profit targets on a profitable short move, leading to potentially missed profit-taking opportunities or suboptimal exit logic.
4.  **Exit Reason Misclassification for Short Trades (Execution Bug):**
    *   **Description:** The reported `exit_reason` for short trades is incorrect relative to the actual price action and the inverted stop/target logic.
    *   **Evidence:**
        *   **TOST:** Reported `partial_exit`. Exit $35.36 (loss for short). This exit is neither at a stop nor a profit target (given targets are misconfigured).
        *   **AMZN:** Reported `stop_hit`. Exit $272.60. For a short entry of $278.72, an exit at $272.60 is a profit. The actual stop for AMZN was $251.76 (which itself is misplaced). The exit price is far from the stop price, and represents a profit, not a stop hit.
    *   **Impact:** Distorts the `Stop-hit rate` (reported 50% vs actual 0% for these trades) and other performance metrics, making it difficult to analyze and improve the strategy's exit discipline.
