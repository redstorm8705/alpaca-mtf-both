## Why this design (verify-at-source findings)

1. **The consumed signal is the scale-INVARIANT label** (sign of net gamma + a ratio), computed
   BEFORE `raw_gex_m` is scaled. A magnitude glitch (the "Monday 100×") cannot flip the label →
   the originally-proposed raw_gex_m scale-anomaly check was **rejected 5-0** (unsound + misdirected;
   live SPY `|raw_gex_m|` ranges 16→510,739 in 5 days, bimodal — a magnitude outlier check would
   blank ~40% of valid readings).
2. **The one uncaught label-corruption path is a plausible-but-wrong SPOT** (bad tick / gap) that
   feeds BS gamma + the moneyness windows and CAN flip the label. STALE + the strike-quality gate do
   not cover a moderate wrong spot. The guard closes it.
3. **Re-arm stays INSIDE the risk envelope** (5-0): the mult is upstream of the 4.5%/trade Kelly
   clamp; 7% kill switch, stops, gross-exposure gate untouched. Aggressiveness within caps, not
   envelope-widening. (Correlated book-wide up-size on a wrong NEGATIVE is the reason the spot guard
   is mandatory and ships in the same diff.)
