1. Entry timing: replay buys the next session's OPEN after the signal close. Live keeps a pending setup at the 55-day level

   possibly days later (BRK.B: replay 2026-10-19 @ 530.00, live 2026-10-27 @ 544.60). Live fill = marketable DAY limit at
   ask + up to 0.2%.
2. Stop distance: replay 2.5 x ATR14 of the 14 bars BEFORE the signal bar. Live 2.5 x ATR14 on closed bars at entry, floored
   at the stock's own 10th-percentile ATR% x price. The ratchet trigger (k x R) inherits whichever R the lot was sized with.
3. Ratchet fill: replay assumes the break-even stop fills at min(open, stop) from daily OHLC; live would be a resting GTC
   stop fed by the SIP/IEX tape (gap-through and queue slippage not modelled). Live has NO ratchet today.
4. Close exits: replay uses the official daily close; live checks the last trade in the final 10 minutes before the close.
5. Universe: replay = fixed list of the 48 symbols with data out of the 2026-10-26 top-50 (survivorship: today's leaders);
   live re-ranks the top 50 daily by 60-day median dollar volume. Replay skips nothing for F6-reserved or wash-trade names.
6. Sizing/caps: replay is in R per trade, one lot per name, unlimited concurrent lots; live caps 10 slots, K-limb overnight
   notional, gap-resize and the 7% kill switch (new entries only).
