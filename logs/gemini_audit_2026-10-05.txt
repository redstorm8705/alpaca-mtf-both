- **`strategy/day_tier_track_m.py` | `evaluate()`**:
  - **Logic Error**: The code calculates `day0 = now_et.replace(hour=0, minute=0...)`. It then fetches bars for Friday using `fetch_bars_window(..., day0 - timedelta(days=10), day0, ...)`.
  - **Failure Condition**: On Monday 2026-10-05, `day0` is 2026-10-05 00:00. The window ends at `day0`. As noted in the code's own comments, Alpaca stamps daily bars at 00:00 ET. This fetch *includes* the Monday bar (which has no close yet or a partial one), causing `d.index[-1]` to potentially be Monday, not Friday. The `_dates` filter `[x < now_et.date() for x in _dates]` attempts to fix this, but `now_et.date()` is Monday.
