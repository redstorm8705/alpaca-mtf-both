2.  **INFRASTRUCTURE | HIGH | Excessive `gc.collect()` duration and Slow Cycle**
    *   **Description**: The bot's execution cycles (`main` loop) are consistently slow, with durations around 580 seconds. A significant portion of this delay is attributed to Python's garbage collector (`gc.collect()`), which takes 3.5 to 4.4 seconds per invocation, accompanied by a "heap may be growing" warning.

    *   **Failure condition**: Inefficient memory management or a potential memory leak is causing the garbage collector to work excessively, leading to significant pauses in the bot's operation.
