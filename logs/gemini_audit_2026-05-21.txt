*   **`execution/entry_logic.py`**
    *   **Logic Error: Max Positions Violation Handling**: In `execute_entries`, at line ~749, the `BUG-POS-1 fix` correctly synchronizes `risk.open_positions` with `len(tracker.open_trades)`. However, if `len(tracker.open_trades)` is `6` (as seen in logs), then `risk.open_positions` is set to `6`. This *syncs* the number but does not *correct* the underlying over-limit state where `MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS=4` (or `5` in power hour).
        *   **Trace**: The runtime correctly sets `risk.open_positions` to `6` because `tracker.open_trades` holds 6 positions. Subsequently, `risk.can_open_position()` (called upstream) will evaluate `6 > config.MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS` (which is 4 or 5) as True, blocking all new entries. The `ANOMALY-2` warning clearly indicates this blocking is active. The bug is allowing 6 positions to exist in `tracker.open_trades` when the maximum allowed is 4 (or 5).
        *   **Failure Condition**: The system allows the accumulation of more open positions than explicitly configured (6 vs. MAX 4 or 5), leading to a violation of risk management rules and an effective halt on new entries until positions are closed.
*   **`execution/portfolio_tracker.py`**
    *   **Logic Error: Cumulative P&L Calculation in `record_partial_exit`**: In `record_partial_exit`, around line ~556, the `_trade["pnl"]` field is updated as `_trade["pnl"] = round(_new_pnl + _trade.get("pnl_remaining", 0.0), 2)`.
        *   **Trace**: `_new_pnl` calculates the profit/loss for the *current tranche being exited*. `_trade["partial_pnl"]` (correctly) accumulates the total realized P&L from all partial exits. `_trade["pnl"]` is typically expected to reflect the *total realized P&L for the trade so far*. By summing `_new_pnl` (current tranche) with `pnl_remaining` (which represents the P&L of *shares still open if exited at a specific price* or is 0), `_trade["pnl"]` does not accurately represent the cumulative realized P&L from all tranches that have been closed. For example, if two partial tranches yield P&L of 50 and 100 respectively, `partial_pnl` will correctly become 150, but `pnl` would show 100 after the second tranche (assuming `pnl_remaining` is 0).
        *   **Failure Condition**: The `pnl` field in `open_trades` (and potentially in some `trade_events` records) does not accurately reflect the total cumulative realized profit/loss after multiple partial exits, leading to misleading internal state for a live trade.
