The lock is not strictly necessary for the `float` assignment itself but is vital if `_last_cycle_complete` were a complex object. Assuming the intent is to guard *reads* by the watchdog during a write, the lock is placed correctly around `_last_cycle_complete = time.time()`. This is likely fine as a defensive measure.
