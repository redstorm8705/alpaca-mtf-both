## Step 0 finding + ledger-freshness design (2026-09-26)
**Step 0 (shadow reconciliation) already exists.** `run_ledger_sync.py` runs every 20 minutes during RTH (crontab `*/20 13-21 * * 1-5`). It rebuilds the per-tier ledger and logs drift per symbol, with a streak alert.

**Production drift:** one run per day on 09-22..25. It is always in the day-tier windows:

| Date | Time | Symbol | Drift |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09-22 | 13:40 UTC (09:40 ET) | UBER | +2 |
| 09-23 | 19:40 UTC (15:40 ET) | GOOGL | +4 |
| 09-24 | 19:40 UTC (15:40 ET) | GOOGL | +3 |
| 09-25 | 19:40 UTC (15:40 ET) | EWY | −5 |

- Each cleared by the next run.
- **Cause [hypothesis — unverified]:** fills landing between the fills and positions fetches.

**Blocking issue for shared symbols:**
- On a PROTECTED symbol, `check_never_sell_floor` (`ownership_guard.py` ~L427-442) REJECTS every sell, including a stop placement or a stop exit, when the ledger sum ≠ live Alpaca net.
- The ledger is up to 20 minutes old. So a swing entry on an F6-held symbol would freeze its own stop until the next sync.

**Design (Gro A→D, GAI B+D, board masked-loss seat D-with-drift-check; combined to satisfy all three):**
- **(B) Synchronous posting of protected-tier fills.** F6 and QHM buys and sells post their confirmed fill to the ledger under the write lock immediately. The protected floor is never stale from the bot's own actions.
- **(A) At-check incremental attribution.** On a mismatch, fetch only the fills since the ledger's last sync (a new recent-fills helper; `pnl_ledger.fetch_all_fills` paginates from inception). Attribute them by `client_order_id` tier. If they exactly explain the gap, proceed with the corrected per-tier quantities.
- **(D′) Unexplained excess counts as protected.** If the gap is still unexplained, bound any sell to live net − (ledger floor + any unexplained positive excess). Page it.
  - A swing stop can still cover the attributed swing shares.
  - Not-yet-recorded shares are never sellable. This closes the seat's F6-just-bought race.

**Tests:**
- drift → proper bound on all three broker paths;
- F6 fill before the ledger write, then a concurrent intraday sell → cannot touch F6 shares;
- swing entry on an F6 symbol → swing stop placed immediately;
- truncated recent-fills fetch → fail closed on protected shares;
- lock serialisation.

**Plan update:** this is step 0b (RP). It is required before step 10 (enforcement ON).
