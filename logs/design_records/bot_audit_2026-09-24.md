## A. Executive diagnosis

1. **The core confluence strategy has no edge in its current form.** Since tagging began in July: 110 trades,
   PF 0.29, win rate 20.9%, avg −0.17R, net −$351. The untagged April–June trades were PF 1.51, but almost all
   of that came in April (+$339) and it decayed monthly after that. The whole account is flat
   ($2,500 → $2,541). Peak was $2,904 and max drawdown was 18.4%.
2. **The cause is structural, not bad luck. The strategy is built for a time horizon it does not trade.**
   - **Correction (Rafael 2026-09-25):** core is a multi-day SWING strategy by design; the term "intraday" is
     retired (it survives only as an internal tier key, `IN-` order prefix). Holding 107/110 trades > 6 h
     (median about 3 days) is therefore intended, not a defect. The day-trade tier is separate and new.
   - Targets are about 2.6× the daily ATR (median 2.08R), but the median best price reached is only +0.26R.
     Just 13% of trades ever reach +1R, and none reach +2R. The targets are effectively unreachable, so every trade ends at a
     stop or a buffer exit.
3. **The exits are not the problem; the entries are.** The overnight buffer exit looks terrible (55 exits, −$368).
   A counterfactual on 31 of those trades shows that holding to the original stop or target would have lost
   more (−$343 vs −$261). The exit saves money.
4. **The scoring does not discriminate between good and bad trades.**
   - A score of 12 did no better than 10.
   - Three of the seven conditions were true on every entry, so they carry no information.
   - momentum_12_1 looks anti-predictive. When true: 63 trades, 13% win, −$273. When false: 16 trades, 56% win.
     The sample is small, so treat this as a hypothesis to test, not a finding.
   - Scoring reads unfinished bars.
5. **The integrity layer can leave a position without protection.** This is P0 and comes before any signal
   work. There are four verified mechanisms (section D). Today's "NAKED GOOGL" alarm was false: the midday audit read
   orders without the nested stop legs. That fix is in flight. The real mechanisms are still there, and they
   produced UBER (204 min without a broker stop), GEV (64 min) and AAPL/HOOD (7 min).
6. **Several risk controls fail open or are never used.**
   - The overnight cap is defined but never read.
   - The gross cap counts only the core book, and it allows the entry if it hits an error.
   - The day-tier kill reports "not tripped" if it hits an error.
   - There is no drawdown halt.
   - Gross exposure is about 150% of equity.

**Bottom line:** integrity first (P0), then decide what the core strategy actually is (P1). The day tier and
quarterly holds are too young to judge. Keep collecting data on them, per Rafael's test-and-learn direction.

## F. Top required changes (ranked)

Each item includes: the problem, current behavior, proposed behavior, files, test, and acceptance. Every item goes
through the full patch sequence. Items touching size, stops or exits are risk-path, so the cold board and
masked-loss seat are mandatory.

**P0-1 — Kill switch blocks entries and keeps protecting.** *Files:* `strategy/run_cycle.py`.
- **Current:** returns before exits.
- **Proposed:** on a trip, block new entries but still run `check_exits`, the stop reconciler and after-hours GTC placement. While
  halted, allow only protective actions: place or restore stops, close positions. No breakeven or trailing adjustments.
  This is GAI's caution, adopted.
- *Gro vs GAI:* Gro wants an immediate full flatten on trip. That is a separate policy choice for Rafael; the 7% switch today halts
  entries and does not flatten. The integrity fix is the same either way.
- *Test:* simulate a trip with 3 open core positions and confirm check_exits runs and a GTC is placed.
- *Accept:* 0 unprotected minutes on a trip day in replay.

**P0-2 — Atomic stop replacement.** *Files:* `execution/lifecycle.py`, `execution/broker.py`, and the partial and
full-close paths.
- **Proposed:** use Alpaca's replace-order (PATCH) where it applies. Otherwise confirm the cancel reached `canceled`,
  then submit the new stop. If that fails, restore the original stop, retry with backoff, and page
  (CRITICAL Slack) on exhaustion.
- *Test:* a mock broker returns 40310000 on the first submit and the stop must still end up in place.
- *Accept:* replaying UBER 9/18 shows 0 minutes without a stop.

**P0-3 — A position-integrity check every RTH cycle.** *New:* `execution/position_integrity.py`, called from
`run_cycle` before entries.
- **Proposed:** every broker position must have an owner (tier) and its designed protection: broker stop, OCO leg,
  or a documented software stop. If protection is missing: restore it, then page, then block that
  tier's entries.
  - A position that cannot be protected after N cycles is flattened.
  - GAI's "flatten everything on any exception" is rejected. A single data hiccup would dump the whole book.
  - Fail closed means blocking entries, not selling everything.
  - This reuses `reporting/broker_ground_truth.classify`.
- *Accept:* the `unmanaged_positions` metric is 0 for 20 consecutive sessions.

**P0-4 — Controls fail closed, and exposure is counted across the whole book.** *Files:* `risk_manager.py`, `run_day_tier.py`,
`entry_logic.py`.
- **Proposed:**
  - An error in any kill check or cap blocks entries.
  - The gross cap sums core + day tier + quarterly holds.
  - Enforce `MAX_OVERNIGHT_EXPOSURE_PCT` at the pre-close sweep.
  - Add a drawdown halt: RED at 12% from peak, KILL at 15%.
- *Accept:* unit tests with injected exceptions all block.

**P0-5 — One stop, used for both sizing and enforcement.** *Files:* `entry_logic.py`, `risk_manager.py`, `run_cycle.py` (after-hours GTC).
- **Proposed:** compute the stop once at sizing, persist it, and enforce exactly that stop. R always uses the original stop.
- *Accept:* for each entry, sizing_stop == enforced_stop, logged in the decision stack.

**P1-6 — Make the swing strategy's entries and exits fit its real move size.** This is research, not a patch. *(Superseded framing — see the BGGN record `bggn_tp_sizing_dynamic_limits_2026-09-25.md`.)*
- Walk-forward test (purged, per LdP) of two designs on 2025–2026 bars:
  - (a) [withdrawn — core is swing by design]
  - (b) designed swing: daily bars, daily-ATR stop and target sized to real MFE, overnight gap budget.
- Pick one only if its out-of-sample PF is ≥1.2 with n≥100.
- *Until then:* **my recommendation is to pause new core entries** (GAI said the same). Rafael decides.
  - Under the North-Star mandate the case is not about preserving capital. A PF 0.29 book has negative expected growth, and the
    money it loses is budget that the day tier and quarterly holds could use.
  - Pausing entries only, not exits, is bounded and reversible.

**P1-7 — Signal hygiene.**
- Use completed bars only.
- Drop the three always-true conditions.
- Test momentum_12_1's sign out of sample.
- Re-derive weights on held-out data.
- Map score to size monotonically, so 11 and 12 no longer get identical size.

**P1-8 — Operating contract and states (section J), enforced in code.** The scorecard computes from fills nightly. RED and KILL act
automatically. This is not just a report banner.

**P2-9 — Day tier stays test-and-learn with its own scorecard.**
- Fix its fail-open kill (covered by P0-4).
- Wire the planned Track A/B sub-kill once B has traded.
- Judge it at n≥100.

**P2-10 — Brief the audit AIs on each tier** (Proposal 2, already approved; see below). This stops recommendations to shut the day tier down and
false "ignoring confluence" claims.
