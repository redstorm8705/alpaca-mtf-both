This design moves from "static confluence" to a "regime-aware probabilistic" framework. The low Spearman correlation (0.02) confirms your current features suffer from **multicollinearity**—they measure the same trend, giving a false sense of security.

### (a) SWING Feature Set (Daily/Weekly/4H)
Drop all 12 current points. Replace with features measuring distinct alpha sources:
1.  **Residual Momentum (Blitz/Huij):** Return of stock minus sector/market return (20d, 60d, 250d).
2.  **52-Week High Proximity (George-Hwang):** Current Price / 52-week High (continuous).
3.  **Frog-in-the-Pan (Da):** Cumulative log returns with low daily volatility (price "creeping" up vs. "gapping").
4.  **IV Skew / Put-Call Signal (Cremers-Weinbaum):** Measure option market sentiment relative to underlying price.
5.  **Industry Relative Strength:** Rank of sector momentum vs. universe.
6.  **Kaufman Efficiency Ratio (ER):** Measures "trendiness" vs. "noise" (20d).

### (b) DAY-TIER (15-min)
Shift to **Relative Volume (RVOL) + GEX pinning**.
*   **Setup:** Stocks in top 5% of RVOL (3-month baseline) during the first 60 minutes.
*   **Trigger:** Expansion from 15-min opening range + GEX (Gamma Exposure) proximity. If positive GEX, prioritize breakouts; if negative GEX, prioritize mean-reversion around VWAP.

### (c) Decision Engine: Meta-Labeling
Avoid "Fixed Scores."
*   **Model:** Use a **Random Forest meta-labeler** (easier to interpret than GBM for small data).
*   **Target:** Binary classification (Triple-Barrier: Profit target = 2x Volatility, Stop Loss = 1x Volatility, Time limit = 10 bars).
*   **Execution:** The primary model predicts *direction*; the meta-labeler predicts *probability of success*. If Prob(Success) < 0.60, no entry.

### (d) Regime Layer
Use a **2-State HMM (Hidden Markov Model)**:
*   **Inputs:** VIX level + VIX/VIX3M term structure + Breadth (TraderMonty index).
*   **Conditioning:** 
    *   *Regime 0 (Low Vol/Risk-On):* Long-only, focus on Momentum/52-week highs.
    *   *Regime 1 (High Vol/Risk-Off):* Neutralize, shift to mean-reversion/protective puts or cash. 
*   **Impact:** The meta-labeler coefficients are re-weighted based on the HMM state.

### (e) Retrain Loop
*   **Daily:** Update HMM state and meta-labeler coefficients (incremental fit).
*   **Weekend:** Full re-run of universe.
*   **Validation:** Use **Purged K-Fold Cross-Validation**.
*   **Drift Monitor:** **Population Stability Index (PSI)** on input features. If PSI > 0.2, trigger "Stale Model" alert; halt bot.
*   **Acceptance:** Backtest must exceed a **Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR)** hurdle of 2.0. If validation fails, revert to previous "Gold" model state (version control via Git).

### (h) Biggest Failure Mode: "The Noise-Fitting Trap"
**Failure:** Fitting 6-12 features to only 46 trade outcomes (overfitting).
**Prevention:** 
*   Use **feature selection** (Permutation Importance) to prune features that don't add to the Information Coefficient (IC).
*   If the model complexity > sample size/10, aggressively regularize.
*   *Alternative rejected:* Deep Learning/RNN. *Why?* Requires massive data to converge. With limited labels, a simpler tree-based model is more robust against "hallucinated" patterns.

**Uncertainty Flag:** The 4-hour timing layer is the "middle ground" of volatility. It is susceptible to "choppy drift." Expect high noise in this timeframe during sideways market regimes.
