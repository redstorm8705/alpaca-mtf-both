## 1. Alpaca facts (sources: docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca, /245-trading-for-trading-api, /paper-trading, read 2026-10-29)
- Extended hours accept ONLY `type=limit` with `time_in_force` `day` or `gtc` and `extended_hours=true`. Market, stop and
  stop-limit are "No" for both DAY and GTC; IOC/FOK/OPG/CLS are "No". GTC + extended hours IS supported (current docs).
- Stops do not trigger outside 09:30-16:00 ET ("Trailing stop will not trigger outside of the regular market hours"; stop
  and stop-limit are not extended-hours eligible). A resting GTC stop below a gap therefore converts to market on the
  first regular-session consolidated print — exactly 06:30 PT. That is the mechanism behind "6:31".
- Sessions: overnight 20:00-04:00 ET (Blue Ocean ATS, limit only, DAY/GTC, `overnight_tradable` asset flag, 2x margin max,
  no DTBP), pre-market 04:00-09:30, after-hours 16:00-20:00 ET. Trading API accounts are enabled for 24/5 by default.
- Paper: fills against NBBO, no queue position, random 10% partial fills, quantity not checked against displayed size;
  paper-only accounts are entitled to IEX data. Extended hours are listed as available in paper; the page does not
  describe how extended-hours orders are simulated (unknown — must be probed on the paper account before calling it live).
- `broker.submit_limit_order(..., extended_hours=True, time_in_force="gtc")` already exists (broker.py L717-809);
  `broker.replace_stop_order` (L1775, Alpaca PATCH replace) exists — a stop price can be moved without a cancel gap.

### Diagnosis (why neither beats the open reliably)
- The loss is the gap, not the exit time: median gap 421 bps; exit-policy differences average ±20-40 bps.
- Earnings gaps keep drifting down after the open (post-earnings drift): waiting cost −40 bps mean in test, 61% worse.
  Non-earnings gaps (market or sector shocks) partly mean-revert in the first 30 min: waiting gained +28 bps in test.
- The profitable formula on this data: **earnings/news gap → exit at the open (without paying a market order's open
  spread); non-earnings gap → W2**. Earnings risk itself was replayed in `earnings_risk_1029.md` (holding through
  reports at full size earned the most), so the cure is not avoiding reports but not paying the market-at-open spread.

## 3. Recommended policy (for owner decision)
1. **Pre-open re-arm (all GTC-stop tiers: Swing, QHM; F6 never sells; Day tier is flat overnight).** From 09:00 ET
   (06:00 PT) the bot reads the SIP/IEX pre-market price. If it is below the position's stop, it moves the resting GTC
   stop DOWN to the catastrophe floor with `replace_stop_order` (one PATCH, no stopless instant) so nothing sells at the
   first print. Floor = premarket price − F·ATR14, F = rolling 75th percentile of the universe's post-open drawdown on
   gap days (ATR units; 0.51 on 2020-23), recomputed weekly; a stock with ≥ 30 own gap events uses its own.
2. **After the open, non-earnings gap: W2** — limit-sell half when price retraces 15% of the gap (C − O), the rest at
   07:00 PT or the floor, whichever first; the remaining stop is restored to the floor after each partial.
3. **After the open, earnings/news gap (F1 detector or FMP date ≤ 3 days): sell by marketable limit in the first 5-min
   bar** (limit = max(bid, O − ½ spread)) instead of letting the stop go market — owner's "not at 6:31" overridden by data
   only if he agrees; alternative is W2 for all at an expected −40 bps (≈ −$4 per $1,000 held) on earnings gaps.
4. **Extended-hours path (owner request, built but bounded):** an `extended_hours=true` GTC limit sell is used for (a)
   the catastrophe floor outside RTH — if AH/pre-market trades below the FLOOR (not the stop) the bot sends a marketable
   ext-hours limit, since stops cannot fire there; (b) scale-out into a pre-market bounce above the prior stop. It does
   NOT sell on the first after-hours print below the normal stop (replay: no edge, ~40% of such days recover).
5. If the bot is down at 06:25 PT the resting stop stays where it is (fails to today's behaviour, never stopless).

Worst book days in the panel (sum across positions gapping the same day): 2022-10-13 −1,095 bps over 4 names,

**Changes:**
1. **Book-level re-arm budget (Taleb 1).** Before any re-arm, Σ over gapped lots of (original stop − floor) × qty must
   fit within today's remaining kill-switch headroom (7% × start-of-day equity − today's realized loss − the open
   stop-risk of every other position). Over budget → the deepest gaps (largest (stop − floor) × qty first) take the
   open exit until it fits. The replay reports the worst book day in equity terms.
2. **The original stop stays the risk basis (Taleb 2, Harris D2).** The floor is a separate field (`gap_floor_px`);
   R, Kelly and post-mortems use the original stop. The tier's own state is the single writer: persist intent
   (floor, original stop, staged client_order_id) → move the stop → adopt the new order id via `resolve_live_order` →
   save stop_order_id + floor together. QHM's coverage reconciler compares against the floor while a gap exit is
   active, so it never "repairs" the stop back up.
3. **Moving the stop pre-open (Harris D1).** First a read-only paper probe: is a resting GTC stop in `accepted`
   replaceable before 09:30? If not, re-arm = cancel-confirmed + a new GTC stop at the floor, read back before the
   tier's next cycle; if not confirmed, resubmit the original stop and page. A stop cannot fire before 09:30, so the
   brief stopless step carries no fill risk as long as it is closed and read back.
4. **No resting bounce limit (Harris D3).** When the last trade touches the bounce level: resize the stop to the
   remaining qty (`replace_stop_order(qty=)`), send a marketable limit for the sold part, settle it, re-grow the stop
   for any unfilled shares. Co-held names: each tier changes only its own tagged stop; the F6 never-sell floor check
   must pass. One broker action per cycle (Swing runs on the run_cycle thread — corrects Q28).
5. **Earnings branch (Taleb 3, Harris D4, GAI 1).** Earnings status unknown (no FMP date and no F1 read) → open exit,
   never the wait branch. An owner-editable earnings override file is checked first (GAI). The sale is a marketable
   limit: limit = last trade − a collar derived from that name's recent opening spreads (Schwab NBBO when available,
   IEX otherwise), repriced one step per cycle; the floor stop stays as the backstop.
6. **Time-limit exit** by marketable limit (same collar), not market. "O" = the first regular-session trade (halts).
7. **Pre-market already below the floor (GAI 3)** → no waiting: exit at the first regular print by marketable limit.
8. **Ordering (Taleb 6):** a circuit-breaker / safe_close_all flatten takes precedence; gap_exit stands down.
9. **Reversal criterion, tail limb (Taleb 7):** any day whose total loss beyond the ORIGINAL stops exceeds the re-arm
   budget, or the first 20 gapped positions averaging below the open by more than 50 bps → policy set to open-exit for
   all names (kill flag `GAP_EXIT_ENABLED`) and the owner is told.
10. **Logging:** each `gap_exit` stage logs replace status, old and new order ids, collar, earnings source (FMP /
    F1 / override), and P&L against entry, against the original stop and against the SIP open.
11. **Simulation list additions:** paper replace-in-`accepted` probe; replace-then-fill with the tier state reconciled;
    split half-sale with a floor hit; co-held QHM + Swing; safe_close_all on the same morning.
- Still fixed (flagged for the build to derive or justify): the 0.75 floor quantile (an estimator input), the ½ split,

**Changes:**

  loss − Σ gapped lots (prior close − first regular print) × qty − the open stop-risk of every other position; the
  re-arm charges Σ (first print − floor) × qty against it. Over budget → the deepest gaps take the open exit. The
  worst-book-day figure is computed in dollars on the live book at build (the replay's equal-weight figure stands only
  as a shape check).
- **Floor anchor = the first regular-session print (Taleb 5).** Before the open the stop is moved to a provisional
  floor (pre-market price − F·ATR, which cannot fire before 09:30); at the first cycle after the open it is set to

- **Crash-between-move-and-save test (Harris 3):** the Swing build carries a test that a stop moved at the broker but

## 4. Safety envelope
- "A stop on every position": the stop is moved, never cancelled (PATCH replace); failure of the replace leaves the
  original stop → today's behaviour. Floor is always a resting GTC stop during RTH.
- Kill switch (7%, fills + prior close): the gapped position's mark counts either way; the kill switch halts NEW entries
  only, and gap-exit sells are reductions so they are never blocked by it. Waiting can raise the realized loss on a
  floor hit (W2 worst −453 bps of the position); at the Swing ≤ 2%-of-equity-at-stop sizing that is < 1% of equity.
- Never-mask-a-loss: P&L from Alpaca fills only; partial exits recorded per fill.
- `paper=True`, data tiers unchanged (T1 Alpaca SIP/IEX for pre-market price; FMP T2 for earnings date).
